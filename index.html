Qubit Entropy & The Casimir Effect

Theoretical Physics

Extending the Qubit Entropy formalism to analyze the Casimir effect for a spherical shell, specifically exploring the mathematical predictors for the change of sign.

Early Universe Thermodynamics

ISI Kolkata

Wrangled coupled Boltzmann equations for photon and neutrino temperatures. Used Python’s ODE solvers to integrate the Liouville equation (T = 10 MeV to 10⁻² MeV) to compute Neff factoring in QED corrections.

Ion Trajectory Kinematics

AMPL, IISER Bhopal

Simulated chaotic particle behavior in a COLTRIMS setup using SIMION, Python, and Lua. Automated the tracking of momentum correlation maps for sequential tri-atomic fragmentation.

Gravitational Collapse Dynamics

General Relativity

In-depth study of Oppenheimer and Snyder's 1939 paper on gravitational collapse, analyzing the metric transitions and foundational math behind continuous contraction.
