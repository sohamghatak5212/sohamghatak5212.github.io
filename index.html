    a fever dream

    For some reason unknown, I decided to typeset our lectures in LaTeX. What began as an earnest attempt at academic heroism—complete with a solemn vow to never skip a single algebraic step—quickly spiraled into a tangled mess of equations, typos, and passive-aggressive comments to my future self. What you’ll find here are the surviving remnants of that noble quest: a ragtag collection of notes I managed to compile before reality (and deadlines) intervened. Please note that these pages are not blessed by the lecturers, the physics gods, or any remotely credible entity. Any conceptual blunders, rogue minus signs, or unphysical conclusions are entirely mine—and possibly the fault of the voices in my head during exam week. Still, if you're looking for a guide to what we covered (complete with references and the occasional cry for help), I hope these are useful. If you spot any errors, please shoot me an email! I’ll be more than happy to fix them.
